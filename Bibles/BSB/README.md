# Berean Standard Bible

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), dedicated to the public domain in April 2023 ("This text of God's Word has been dedicated to the public domain", https://bereanbible.com/bsb.txt).

Source: eBible.org engbsb VPL (https://ebible.org/Scriptures/engbsb_vpl.zip, about page: "Public Domain"), converted by scripts/import-bibles.ts into the KJV's books, format and verse numbering. Verses the BSB leaves out (Matthew 17:21 and others) are empty strings.

One correction to the source: Psalm 3:1 reads "A Psalm of David" (eBible's text has the typo "A Psalms of David").
