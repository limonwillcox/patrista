## Axes

A pair of opposite poles that a story moves along.

### Heaven and earth  `heaven-earth`

The first division of creation. Heaven is the pattern, meaning and source; earth is the matter that receives it and is shaped by it.

**Poles:** heaven / earth
**Key texts:** Gen 1:1; Exod 25:40; Matt 6:10; Heb 8:5
**Christ:** Christ joins heaven and earth in his own person (John 1:51; Eph 1:10).
**Where the readings differ:** Jordan: heaven is the blueprint and earth the copy. Pageau: heaven is meaning and earth is matter, joined in knowing. The Fathers: what is above gives its quality to what is below.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Heaven as blueprint for earth): A vertical axis on which heaven is the archetype and earth the copy, so earthly order is modelled on heaven's pattern. *ch. 4, p. 42*
- **Jordan**, TNE (The world as God's house): The whole cosmos is built as God's dwelling; heaven is His finished throne-house and earth is meant to become one. *ch. 4, p. 49*
- **Pageau**, talks (Heaven (principle) / earth (matter)): The basic polarity of reality. Heaven is invisible meaning, principle and identity. Earth is tangible matter, multiplicity and potential. *pp. 52–53; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1-vG-bLDwE; https://adversariapodcast.com/2019/03/07/transcript-for-matthieu-pageau-the-language-of-creation/*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('heaven' (upper half of the cosmos)): The upper, spiritual half of reality: pure meaning without body, the source of identity, principle and light. *ch. 5*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('earth' (lower half of the cosmos)): The lower, bodily half of reality: matter without meaning, dark enigmas waiting to host and express a principle. *ch. 5*
- **Pageau**, LoC (union of heaven and earth): Heaven covering earth with meaning while earth supports and expresses heaven; Pageau's basic pattern behind every phenomenon. *ch. 5*
- **Fathers** (Above and below): What is above gives its quality to what is below; earthly signs come first and carry heavenly effects. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 4 — “an underlying material substance should catch the quality of that which overhangs it”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 3 — “the material substance which governs terrestrial life acts as agent likewise in the celestial.”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 10 — “"He who is from the earth speaketh concerning the earth; He who comes from the realms above is above all;"”*

</details>

### One and many  `one-many`

Movement between scattered particulars and the single identity that gathers them. Going up gathers; falling scatters.

**Poles:** one / many
**Key texts:** Gen 11:1-9; Acts 2:1-11; John 17:21
**Christ:** Pentecost undoes Babel: many tongues, one Spirit (Acts 2).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (The one and the many): Movement between scattered particulars below and one gathering identity above. Ascent gathers into unity, and falling scatters into multiplicity. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/a-lecture-by-jonathan-pageau-the-symbolic-world-ep-206*
- **Pageau**, talks (Light, air, water, earth): A four-tier vertical cosmos. Tiers rise from heavy, dark and many (earth) to light, bright and one (light), following Genesis 1:2–3. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/the-language-of-creation-matthieu-pageau-ep-292*
- **Pageau**, LoC (light, air, water, earth): Vertical space ranked by density and brightness: the higher, the rarer, clearer and simpler; the lower, the heavier, darker and more common. *ch. 11*

</details>

### Center and margin  `center-margin`

Every ordered world has a center where heaven meets earth, and an edge where the strange, the foreign and the dangerous live.

**Poles:** center / margin
**Key texts:** Gen 2:9; Lev 19:9-10; Num 15:38; Heb 13:12-13
**Christ:** Christ suffers outside the gate, at the margin, and becomes the center (Heb 13:12; Ps 118:22).
**Where the readings differ:** Pageau treats the margin as necessary and not simply evil: a healthy order leaves room for it. Jordan maps the same layout as sanctuary, homeland and outlying world.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Garden, Eden, world): The horizontal world: sanctuary (meet God), homeland (rest), outlying lands (work). It is the three-decker world laid out on the ground. *ch. 12, p. 153*
- **Pageau**, talks (Center and edge): The world is ordered around a center of identity and meaning. Potential, chaos, the foreign and death lie toward the edge. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ddf-9DXASQ*
- **Pageau**, talks (The edge / fringe): The boundary zone that frames an order and marks its limit. It is linked with death, monsters, strangers, the hybrid and the left hand. *https://orthodoxartsjournal.org/jordan-peterson-religious-symbolism-and-popular-culture/; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WCFLbmAJ2Uc:*
- **Pageau**, LoC (the center / middle): Where heaven and earth truly meet: knowledge that is neither too simple nor too complex; the stable middle of the world (Canaan, the garden's tree). *ch. 11*
- **Pageau**, LoC (periphery / margins / edge): The unstable ring around stable space where strange and ironic things happen: the sea, wilderness, fringes, gleanings, outsiders. *ch. 31*

</details>

### Going down and coming up  `descent-ascent`

The vertical movement of a story: down into water, earth, sleep or death, and back up into life, light and heaven.

**Poles:** descent / ascent
**Key texts:** Gen 28:12; Jonah 2:6; Matt 3:16; Eph 4:9-10; Rom 6:4
**Christ:** He descended into the lower parts of the earth and ascended far above the heavens (Eph 4:9-10).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Three-decker universe): The vertical world: heaven above, land in the middle, sea or abyss below. Morally it is heaven, earth and hell. *ch. 12, p. 145*
- **Pageau**, talks (Going up and going down): Vertical movement. Ascent tames, gathers and approaches meaning. Descent goes into waters, earth, death, exile and the foreign. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_Rnd4fJ6Bk; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ddf-9DXASQ*
- **Fathers** (Going down, coming up): The vertical movement of going down (water, earth, Hades) and coming back up (life, heaven), by Christ and in baptism. *Cyril, *Cat.* 3.12 — “Having gone down dead in sins, thou comest up quickened in righteousness.”; Cyril, *Cat.* 14.18 — “who descended into hell alone, but ascended thence with a great company”; Cyril, *Myst.* 2.4 — “descended three times into the water, and ascended again”*
- **Fathers** (Down into the water, up into life): Go down into water as into a grave (three times), come up as from the tomb, newborn. *Cyril, *Myst.* 2.4 — “descended three times into the water, and ascended again; here also hinting by a symbol at the three days burial of Christ.”; Cyril, *Myst.* 2.4 — “in the descent, as in the night, ye saw nothing, but in ascending again ye were as in the day.”; Cyril, *Myst.* 2.5 — “our imitation was in a figure, and our salvation in reality.”*

</details>

### Darkness and light  `light-darkness`

The movement from dark to light, evening to morning, night to day. Darkness is unformed or hidden; light is meaning made clear.

**Poles:** darkness / light
**Key texts:** Gen 1:2-5; Isa 9:2; John 1:5; 2 Cor 4:6
**Christ:** The light shines in the darkness (John 1:5); the resurrection comes at dawn on the first day.
**Where the readings differ:** Jordan: the first darkness is unfinished, not evil. The Fathers often use darkness for the devil's region (west) in baptismal rites.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (From evening to morning): The day moves from dark to light; in history the Old Covenant is night (ruled by the moon) and the New Covenant is day (the Sun's rising). *ch. 5, p. 54*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('light'): Meaning made explicit; the principle that answers the enigma of matter. Created by God's first spoken word. *ch. 10*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('darkness'): Matter cut off from a principle: an obscure question that can still host meaning once answered. *ch. 10*
- **Pageau**, talks (Potential first, then light): Each creative step states the lower, dark or puzzling potential first, then answers it from above with light, word and identity. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ddf-9DXASQ*
- **Pageau**, LoC (evening and morning): The first day's rhythm: light goes down into darkness (evening) and is raised again (morning). Flesh comes in the evening and bread in the morning. (Inferred pairing of ch. 16 and ch. 31.) *ch. 16*
- **Fathers** (Night to day): Movement from darkness or night to light or day, enacted in the descent and ascent of baptism. *Cyril, *Myst.* 2.4 — “in the descent, as in the night, ye saw nothing, but in ascending again ye were as in the day.”; Cyril, *Myst.* 1.4 — “the West is the region of sensible darkness”; Gregory, *Bapt. Chr.*, l.436 — “For as darkness is dispelled by light, and black disappears as whiteness is spread over it, so the old man also disappears”*

</details>

### Order and flux (Pageau's space and time)  `order-flux`

Stable order that holds things apart (dry land, law, work) against change that dissolves and renews (waters, rest, cycles).

**Poles:** order / flux
**Key texts:** Gen 1:6-10; Gen 8:22; Exod 20:8-11
**Where the readings differ:** This is Pageau's frame. Jordan has no single equivalent; his nearest terms are the land-sea line and the work-rest week.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, LoC ('space'): The stabilizing power that holds opposites apart and makes a center where facts match meaning. It is built by work and law. *ch. 31*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('time'): The cosmic cause of cyclical change that turns things into their opposites. It brings death and renewal, and holds the periphery against space. *ch. 31*
- **Pageau**, talks (Flood/time vs dry land/space): Time is the changing, dissolving, renewing force (waters, wheel, rest). Space is the stabilizing, structuring force (dry land, pillar, work). *p. 141; https://adversariapodcast.com/2019/03/07/transcript-for-matthieu-pageau-the-language-of-creation/; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxltkn90Qag*
- **Pageau**, LoC (separating / establishing space): Holding opposites apart by removing the intermediate states, so that certainty replaces confusion. Any separation (milk from meat, linen from wool) "establishes space". *ch. 37*
- **Pageau**, LoC (mixture / impurity / hybridization): Taking in elements foreign to an identity or confusing opposites. Each step yields "less of itself, more of the other" and summons the flood. *ch. 36*
- **Pageau**, LoC (work): Building space: carrying out a plan in matter and integrating raw matter into familiar order. It is tiring, artificial and productive. *ch. 41*
- **Pageau**, LoC (rest / idleness / sleep): Letting things slide back toward primitive conditions (wilderness, flood, sleep, death). It is destructive and also re-creative. *ch. 41*

</details>

### Clean and unclean  `clean-unclean`

The line between what may come near God and what may not, drawn by touch, food, feet and boundaries.

**Poles:** clean / unclean
**Key texts:** Lev 11; Acts 10:9-16; Mark 7:19
**Christ:** In Christ the line moves from foods to hearts (Mark 7:19; Acts 10).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Clean and unclean): A symbolic line between animals (and so people), set by feet and contact: split hooves and cud, scales and fins, careful landing. *ch. 8, p. 101*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Creeping things (dust-dwellers)): Animals of the dirt, linked after the curse with the serpent and evil men; swarming house-invaders are unclean. *ch. 8, p. 100*
- **Pageau**, LoC (forbidden / unclean food, intoxication): Matter the identity cannot integrate. It becomes rebel members in the body and splits head from body ("intoxication"); kosher food is its opposite. *ch. 58*

</details>

### Blessing and curse  `blessing-curse`

Every symbol has a blessed form and a cursed form: tree and thorn, fruit and barrenness, upright and crawling.

**Poles:** blessing / curse
**Key texts:** Gen 3:17-18; Deut 28; Judg 9:8-15; Matt 27:29
**Christ:** Christ wears the crown of thorns: he takes the curse of the ground on his head (Matt 27:29; Gal 3:13).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Grace against wrath): A pole running from blessing (tree, fruit, canopy, upward) to curse (thorn, barren, crawling, downward). *SM p.2*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Thorns and brambles): The cursed ground's ugly growth, standing for the unrighteous: Cain to Abel's tree, and Jotham's bramble-king. *ch. 7, p. 81*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Thorn as cursed tree): A tree in cursed form: it creeps on the ground, bears no fruit and shows God's wrath; also a type of the wicked man. *SM p.2*
- **Fathers** (Thorns): The curse on the ground ("thorns and thistles"), which Christ takes on Himself as a crown. *Cyril, *Cat.* 13.18 — “For this cause Jesus assumes the thorns, that He may cancel the sentence”; Cyril, *Cat.* 13.18 — “for this cause also was He buried in the earth, that the earth which had been cursed might receive the blessing instead of a curse.”*

</details>

### Grasping and receiving  `seize-receive`

Whether a gift, office or knowledge is grabbed early or received from God in its time.

**Poles:** seize / receive
**Key texts:** Gen 3:6; Gen 9:22; Gen 27; Phil 2:6-8
**Christ:** Christ did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped (Phil 2:6).
**Where the readings differ:** Jordan (robe of office taken too early) and Pageau (knowledge taken in all at once) reach the same axis by different roads.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Grasping against receiving): Whether office comes by grasping it early or by waiting for God to give it. *RTD p.52*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Youth against maturity in wisdom): Growth over time in wisdom gained through obedience; office belongs to the mature, and sin also "matures." *RTD p.73*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Usurper who grabs office before time): One who takes judicial office or rule before God gives it, and so becomes a slave or beast. *RTD p.51–52*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (One who waits for God's investiture): The faithful person who has the promise but waits, sometimes for generations, for God to give the robe. *RTD p.61*
- **Pageau**, talks (Grasping everything at once): The serpent's temptation is to take in total knowledge immediately rather than gradually. It ends in wrongful incorporation, nakedness and expulsion. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/the-language-of-creation-matthieu-pageau-ep-292*

</details>

### Elder and younger  `older-younger`

Two brothers: often an earthly, strong elder and a heavenly, weaker younger. The blessing passes to the younger.

**Poles:** elder / younger
**Key texts:** Gen 4; Gen 25:23; Gen 48:14; Luke 15:11-32
**Christ:** Jordan: the elder is a type of the first Adam, the younger of the second Adam.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Firstborn against younger): Which son inherits; in Genesis the failing firstborn is often passed over for a younger son. *RTD p.65*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Younger son who supplants): The younger brother who replaces the older heir. The older is a type of Adam, the younger a type of the Second Adam. *ch. 14, p. 188*
- **Pageau**, talks (Power vs authority brothers): Paired brothers: an earthly one (farmer, hunter, king, "power") and a heavenly one (shepherd, priest, "authority"). Their conflict repeats Cain and Abel. *p. 107; https://adversariapodcast.com/2019/03/07/transcript-for-matthieu-pageau-the-language-of-creation/*
- **Pageau**, LoC (farmer, king, builder (Cain, Esau, Ham)): Adam specialized toward earth: the power that holds land, builds cities and forges tools. He should host the heavenly twin, but often kills or exposes him. *ch. 25*
- **Pageau**, LoC (shepherd, priest (Abel, Jacob, Shem)): Adam specialized toward heaven: contemplative authority that offers refined flesh. Without the earthly twin he is "just breath" (Abel = vanity). *ch. 25*

</details>

### Figure and fulfillment  `figure-fulfillment`

The time axis of typology: an earlier, bodily figure and a later, fuller reality. Repetition is never identical; the later is greater.

**Poles:** figure / fulfillment
**Key texts:** 1 Cor 10:1-11; Col 2:17; Heb 10:1; Luke 24:27
**Christ:** All the figures point to Christ: 'beginning with Moses and all the Prophets' (Luke 24:27).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Restoring type and symbol): Each Old Covenant world foreshadows the New. Jesus and the apostles restored the symbolic reading that had collapsed into moralism (Pharisees) or allegory (Alexandria). *ch. 18, p. 265*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Successive imprints of the heavenly pattern): History as a series of copies of heaven, each more glorious: Tabernacle, Temple, Ezekiel's Temple, New Jerusalem; first Adam to Last Adam. *ch. 4, p. 49*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Spiral time): Biblical time is linear and rhythmical. Patterns repeat with variation and increasing glory, so history moves in spirals, not circles. *ch. 13, p. 169*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Fulfilment as transformation): The new covenant does not abolish the old but brings it to an unseen fullness. The difference is one of glory, not kind. *ch. 18, p. 260*
- **Leithart**, talks (Same shape, new key): A pattern recurs in a later event, changed by its new place in time. A type and its antitype are similar but never identical. *https://theopolisinstitute.com/leithart_post/nonidentical-repetition/*
- **Fathers** (Type to antitype (shadow to body)): The time-axis from the earlier, bodily figure to the later, spiritual reality it foreshadows; the later is greater. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 5 — “according to the rule by which things carnal are always antecedent as figurative of things spiritual.”; Cyril, *Myst.* 1.3 — “Now turn from the old to the new, from the figure to the reality.”; Cyril, *Myst.* 2.5 — “our imitation was in a figure, and our salvation in reality.”*

</details>

### Land and sea  `land-sea`

Dry, ordered ground against restless water. The sea is the edge of the world and what threatens to swallow the land.

**Poles:** land / sea
**Key texts:** Gen 1:9-10; Ps 46:2-3; Dan 7:2-3; Rev 21:1
**Christ:** Christ walks on the sea and stills it (Mark 4:39; 6:48); in the new creation there is no more sea (Rev 21:1).
**Where the readings differ:** Jordan: the sea is mainly the Gentile nations. Pageau: the sea is flux and confusion. Both read Rev 21:1 as the end of that division.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Land (Israel's domain)): The dry land is Israel's sphere in Old Covenant imagery; God's people were farmers and herdsmen of the land. *ch. 8, p. 100*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Sea / waters below (the Gentile world)): The sea is generally the Gentile world; sea monsters are Gentile powers, and the New Covenant mission moves out onto it. *ch. 8, p. 100*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Land versus sea (Israel versus nations)): After the Fall, the line between land and sea pictures the line between God's people and the restless ungodly nations. *ch. 12, p. 146*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Sea / abyss): The lowest deck. It gives life, but after the Fall it images the Abyss and the restless Gentile nations that try to swallow the land. *ch. 12, p. 145*
- **Pageau**, LoC (dry land): Space achieved: a stable place where heaven and earth agree, theory matches practice, and God is revealed directly. *ch. 33*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('waters' / flooded land): The fluid medium of change and confusion between meaning and fact; the first stage of creation and what the flood brings back. Any liquid counts. *ch. 32*

</details>

### Male and female  `male-female`

The human form of heaven and earth: one gives seed and word, the other receives, nourishes and brings it to birth.

**Poles:** male / female
**Key texts:** Gen 2:18-24; Eph 5:31-32; Rev 21:2
**Christ:** Christ and the Church are the great mystery behind marriage (Eph 5:32).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (Heaven/earth at human scale): Man and woman repeat heaven and earth: male as seed or principle, female as body, food or earth. Woman also mediates with the margin. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1-vG-bLDwE*
- **Pageau**, LoC (male): Heaven at the scale of sex: the giver of seed and language. In time/space terms, the one who "fixes" the cycle and builds space. *ch. 19*
- **Pageau**, LoC (female / Eve): Earth at the scale of sex: she raises food from her body to flesh out the seed. In time/space terms, the cycle of renewal and recreation. *ch. 71*

</details>

### Right hand and left hand  `right-left`

Right is blessing, strength and accurate action; left is the loose, weak, set-aside side, near the margin.

**Poles:** right / left
**Key texts:** Gen 48:13-20; Ps 110:1; Matt 25:33

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (Right (blessing, tight order) / left (loose, marginal)): The right hand means blessing and tight integration of mind and body. The left means looseness, play, weakness, the margin, the set-aside. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FNwLyv2HC84*
- **Pageau**, LoC (left hand and right hand (and eyes)): Right is the body carrying out the mind accurately (space). Left is clumsy, loose and marginal (time). Society repeats the split in its marginal groups. *ch. 56*

</details>

### North and south  `north-south`

Biblical geography read vertically: the north is height, source and identity; the south (Egypt) is depth, food and the body.

**Poles:** north / south
**Key texts:** Ps 48:2; Isa 14:13; Gen 12:10; Gen 46:3-4
**Where the readings differ:** Jordan: north is the direction of God's throne. Pageau: going up north for identity and down south for goods.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (North (God's throne)): The direction of God's throne and of Eden's source. Men live in the centre and the outlying world lies south. *ch. 12, p. 150*
- **Pageau**, talks (Heights of seed / depths of sustenance): Biblical geography read vertically: north as heights (Japheth, Laban, white, new seed), south as depths (Ham, Edom, red, food), Shem between. *https://adversariapodcast.com/2019/03/07/transcript-for-matthieu-pageau-the-language-of-creation/*
- **Pageau**, LoC (north / heights): Symbolic height: heaven, white, snowy Lebanon, kin and the source of identity or seed. One goes up north for one's origins. *ch. 29*
- **Pageau**, LoC (south / depths (Egypt, Edom, Sheol)): Symbolic depth: earth, red, Egypt, the underworld. One goes down south to gain food, population and goods. *ch. 29*

</details>

### West and east  `east-west`

East is the side of light and Paradise; exile runs east of Eden, and conversion turns from west to east.

**Poles:** west / east
**Key texts:** Gen 2:8; Gen 3:24; Gen 4:16; Ezek 43:2
**Where the readings differ:** In Genesis, going east is going away from Eden; in the baptismal rite (Cyril), facing east is turning to Paradise.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Fathers** (Turning from west to east): West is the region of darkness and the devil; east is the region of light and Paradise; conversion is a turn from one to the other. *Cyril, *Myst.* 1.4 — “Since the West is the region of sensible darkness, and he being darkness has his dominion also in darkness”; Cyril, *Myst.* 1.9 — “a symbol of this was thy turning from West to East, the place of light.”; Ambrose, *De Myst.* 2.7 — “then you turned to the east; for he who renounces the devil turns to Christ, and beholds Him face to face.”*

</details>

### Head and body  `head-body`

The head gives meaning and direction; the body gives strength and carries it out. Societies are bodies with heads.

**Poles:** head / body
**Key texts:** Dan 2:32-38; 1 Cor 12:12-27; Eph 1:22-23
**Christ:** Christ is head of the body, the Church (Col 1:18). Tyconius' first rule: head and body can speak as one person.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Body and body politic): The parts of one human body match the members and offices of a society, because God is both One and Three. *ch. 2, p. 25*
- **Pageau**, LoC (head / chief): The first principle in a body, a society or an empire; the source of meaning that answers the body's dark needs, while the limbs give power. *ch. 15*
- **Pageau**, talks (Head as principle, crown as what rises above it): The head is the first principle giving meaning to the body. A crown is a transformation set above the head, extending it without destroying it. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/a-lecture-by-jonathan-pageau-the-symbolic-world-ep-206*
- **Pageau**, LoC (rider and mount): Spirit directing power: the rider gives purpose and the mount gives strength. A fallen rider means the hierarchy is overturned. *ch. 17*

</details>

### Garden and city  `garden-city`

History runs from a planted garden to a built city. The city can be the rebel's counterfeit or God's finished house.

**Poles:** garden / city
**Key texts:** Gen 2:8; Gen 4:17; Gen 11:4; Rev 21:2; Rev 22:1-2
**Christ:** The New Jerusalem comes down with the garden and tree of life inside it (Rev 22:1-2).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (From Garden to City): History runs from raw glory to worked glory, from Eden's garden to the New Jerusalem city, as man turns nature into culture. *ch. 10, p. 117*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Cain's city): A city man builds to protect himself instead of hiding in God; the seat of violent, self-enrobed rule. *RTD p.47*
- **Pageau**, talks (Built order of the exiles): An artificial social covering built by those cast out (Cain, Ham, Nimrod). It joins power and technology with an increase of the curse. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gh9BC-wONBs*
- **Pageau**, talks (City with the garden at its center): The final form of the whole pattern: a perfected city descending from heaven, containing the restored garden and tree at its center. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1-vG-bLDwE*

</details>

## Zones

A place, real or cosmic.

### Highest heaven  `highest-heaven`

God's throne-house, made first and unseen, modeled on earth by the Most Holy Place.

**Key texts:** Gen 1:1; 1 Kgs 8:27; Heb 9:24
**Christ:** Christ entered heaven itself, the true holy place (Heb 9:24).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Highest (third) heaven): The heaven of Gen 1:1, made before the earth: God's throne-house where the angels live, invisible to us and the pattern for everything below. *ch. 4, p. 42*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Highest heaven): God's throne-heaven, made on Day One. It is modelled by the Most Holy Place and was closed to man until Christ. *ch. 12, p. 145*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Most Holy Place): The throne room behind the inner veil. It stands for the highest heaven, the cloud-capped summit, and the head or heart. *ch. 15, p. 207*

</details>

### The firmament (the sky)  `firmament`

### The deep (the first waters)  `the-deep`

Waters before or beneath order: dark, unformed, full of possibility. They return in the flood and under every sea crossing.

**Key texts:** Gen 1:2; Gen 7:11; Ps 104:6-9; Jonah 2:5; Rev 20:3
**Christ:** Christ descends to the abyss and rises from it (Rom 10:7). Tertullian: these waters were the first seat of the Spirit and the figure of baptism.
**Where the readings differ:** The traditions do not agree. Jordan: the first deep is unfinished, not evil. Pageau (Matthieu): flux and confusion, not evil in itself. <details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (The deep / the sea): Bottomless primordial waters around the land: unformed potential, chaos, death and the foreign. They return in the flood and the sea crossings. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wITJ3r96hKQ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ddf-9DXASQ:*
- **Pageau**, LoC ('waters' / flooded land): The fluid medium of change and confusion between meaning and fact; the first stage of creation and what the flood brings back. Any liquid counts. *ch. 32*
- **Fathers** (Waters of the beginning): The first waters of creation: unformed, life-bearing, and the "seat" of the Spirit, from which heaven and earth were drawn. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 3 — “water is one of those things which, before all the furnishing of the world, were quiescent with God in a yet unshapen state.”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 3 — “Water was the first to produce that which had life, that it might be no wonder in baptism if waters know how to give life.”; Cyril, *Cat.* 3.5 — “the heavens are from the waters: the earth is the place of men, but the earth is from the waters”*

## Figures

A recurring creature, thing or person-type.

### Ladder between heaven and earth  `world-axis`

Any upright thing that links earth to heaven: tree, mountain, ladder, pillar, altar, staff. Its foot is the gate.

**Key texts:** Gen 28:12-18; Exod 4:2-4; Dan 4:10-12; John 1:51
**Opposite:** `counterfeit-ladder`
**Christ:** 'You will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man' (John 1:51).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Tree as ladder to heaven): A tree as a vertical link between earth and heaven: roots/base as gate, trunk as ladder, leafy crown as the glory-canopy. *ch. 7, p. 84*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Ladder to heaven): Any structure linking earth to heaven: mountain, pyramid, altar, tree, pillar, sanctuary. Every heaven-and-earth model is also one. *ch. 12, p. 161*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Standing stone pillar): Jacob's anointed stone at Bethel. It is a "house of God" and a sign that the cloud will come down on the holy mountain. *ch. 14, p. 191*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Bronze pillars Jachin and Boaz): Two Temple pillars standing for High Priest and King as guardians. Each pillar is a three-tier ladder: shaft, collar, lily. *ch. 16, p. 231*
- **Pageau**, talks (Serpent/ladder path of ascent and descent): A single path that runs both upward and downward between heaven and the abyss, climbed by virtue and fallen down by the passions. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/a-lecture-by-jonathan-pageau-the-symbolic-world-ep-206*
- **Pageau**, LoC (Jacob's ladder): The vertical bridge between heaven and earth where messengers go up and down, exchanging authority for power. It is the spatial axis that branches into four directions. *ch. 30*
- **Pageau**, LoC (cosmic tree / pillar of space): The central pillar that separates above from below and the four directions, and grows consistently from one seed into fruit "according to its kind". *ch. 32*
- **Pageau**, LoC (staff / rod): The cosmic pillar in a hand. It holds space up, leads home from exile and splits waters; released, it becomes a snake. *ch. 64*

</details>

### Counterfeit ladder  `counterfeit-ladder`

A ladder to heaven built up from below by man's own effort: Babel, the ziggurat, the proud tree. God cuts it down.

**Key texts:** Gen 11:4; Isa 14:13-15; Dan 4:20-23
**Opposite:** `world-axis`

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Tower of Babel): A counterfeit holy mountain and ladder, built from cursed ground upward, to seize land, name and priestly mediation. *ch. 12, p. 158*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Descent from above): After the Fall, the true link to heaven must come down from God. Ladders built up from cursed ground are counterfeits. *ch. 12, p. 158*

</details>

### The cross  `cross`

- **Jordan**, TNE (The five sacrificial animals): Ox, sheep, goat, dove and pigeon, the only animals that may be offered; they stand in for human beings. *ch. 8, p. 99*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Garments of skin): God's covering after the Fall: a sign of man's bestial state and a promise of office through a substitute's death. *RTD p.47*
- **Fathers** (The lamb and its blood): The lamb whose blood marks the doors so that the destroyer passes over. *Cyril, *Myst.* 1.3 — “there, the blood of a lamb was the spell against the destroyer; here, the blood of the Lamb without blemish Jesus Christ”; Cyril, *Cat.* 13.19 — “I was like a harmless lamb led to be slaughtered”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 19 — “The Passover affords a more than usually solemn day for baptism”*

</details>

## Roles

A slot in a story that different figures fill.

### Mediator from above  `mediator-from-above`

A heavenly agent that comes down onto the water, the offering or the chosen one and makes it live: Spirit, dove, voice, fire, angel.

**Key texts:** Gen 1:2; Gen 8:8-11; 1 Kgs 18:38; Matt 3:16-17; Acts 2:2-4
**Christ:** At the Jordan: the Spirit as a dove and the Father's voice (Matt 3:16-17).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Spirit hovering over the waters): The Spirit coming from heaven over the deep, bringing heaven's pattern and the first light, so beginning the glorification of creation. *ch. 4, p. 43*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Angels (cosmic stewards and models)): Heaven's created host who run the world for God (weather, motion) and model praise, throne-bearing and guarding for mankind. *ch. 9, p. 107*
- **Pageau**, LoC (angel / messenger): A heavenly carrier of meaning and guidance to humans; to be hosted and fed. Hosted wrongly, angels fall. *ch. 17*
- **Fathers** (One who comes down on the waters from above): A heavenly agent (Spirit, dove, angel, voice, fire) who comes down onto water or a sacrifice and makes it effective. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 4 — “an underlying material substance should catch the quality of that which overhangs it”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 6 — “in the water, under (the witness of) the angel, we are cleansed, and prepared for the Holy Spirit.”; Ambrose, *De Myst.* 5.26 — “When the Holy Spirit also speaks, Who came down in the likeness of a dove?”*
- **Fathers** (Spirit over the waters): The Spirit borne above the waters, who sanctifies whatever water He rests on. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 4 — “the Spirit of God, who hovered over (the waters) from the beginning, would continue to linger over the waters of the baptized.”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 4 — “the Spirit immediately supervenes from the heavens, and rests over the waters, sanctifying them from Himself”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 8 — “Over the waters of baptism, recognising as it were His primeval seat, He reposes”*
- **Fathers** (The Father's voice over the water): A voice from heaven over the waters naming the Son. *Cyril, *Cat.* 3.14 — “a Father's voice sounds over thee from on high--not, "This is My Son," but, "This has now been made My son;"”; Ambrose, *De Myst.* 5.26 — “the Father clearly calls from heaven in the Gospel narrative”; Ambrose, *De Sacr.* 1.5.19 — “Also God the Father spake from heaven. Thou hast the presence of the Trinity.”*

</details>

### Man between heaven and earth  `human-mediator`

The human whose task is to stand between heaven and earth: to bring heaven's pattern down and lift creation up.

**Key texts:** Gen 1:26-28; Gen 2:15; Ps 8:5-6; 1 Tim 2:5
**Christ:** One mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus (1 Tim 2:5).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Man, God's agent of glorification): Man stands between heaven and earth at the top of the mountain and turns nature into culture after the heavenly pattern. *ch. 10, p. 117*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Man, the image and symbol-maker): Man is God's special symbol and viceroy, the only symbol that also makes symbols, and he rules creation by naming and dominion. *ch. 3, p. 31*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Man as God's image and symbol-maker): Humanity as God's primary, special symbol: the one created symbol that also makes symbols. *SM p.1*
- **Pageau**, talks (Man at the center): The human figure who stands between heaven and earth. He brings meaning down into matter and raises matter up, uniting both in himself. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/a-lecture-by-jonathan-pageau-the-symbolic-world-ep-206*
- **Pageau**, LoC (Adam / the human as mediator): Humanity's cosmic job: standing between heaven and earth, lowering meaning and raising matter so that God is known in creation. *ch. 14*

</details>

### Head of a new world  `new-adam`

The one through whom a new world begins after judgment: he repeats Adam's place with more glory.

**Key texts:** Gen 9:1-7; Gen 5:3; Rom 5:12-21; 1 Cor 15:45
**Christ:** The last Adam (1 Cor 15:45). Irenaeus: Christ sums up Adam in himself (recapitulation).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (New Adam): The head of a new world who repeats Adam's place with more glory and office. Examples: Noah, the king, the High Priest, Ezekiel. *ch. 13, p. 174*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (New head who receives the robe): The man who, after the first Adam's failure, receives the robe of office on the basis of a substitute's blood. *RTD p.47*
- **Jordan**, TNE (First and Last Adam (governors of the eras)): The two heads of the Old and New Creations, who are the true rulers of the night-age and the day-age, pictured by moon and sun. *ch. 5, p. 57*
- **Pageau**, LoC (third son / renewed Adam (Seth, Shem, Jacob)): The figure who recovers Adam's image by joining heavenly authority with an earthly inheritance, and who restores balance after the twins' conflict. *ch. 25*
- **Fathers** (Adam and the second Adam): The first man, whose disobedience brought death, and Christ, the second, who replays and reverses his story. *Irenaeus, *AH* 3.18.7 — “as by the disobedience of the one man who was originally moulded from virgin soil, the many were made sinners”; Irenaeus, *AH* 3.21.10 — “recapitulating Adam in Himself, rightly receive a birth, enabling Him to gather up Adam [into Himself], from Mary, who was as yet a virgin.”; Irenaeus, *AH* 3.22.3 — “Hence also was Adam himself termed by Paul "the figure of Him that was to come,"”*

</details>

### The righteous remnant  `remnant`

The one righteous man with his household, or the faithful few, kept alive through judgment to seed a new world.

**Key texts:** Gen 6:8-9; Gen 7:1; 1 Kgs 19:18; Isa 10:20-22; Rom 11:5

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Remnant): The faithful minority who keep worship going in a time of apostasy. *ch. 16, p. 237*
- **Pageau**, LoC (grace): Perfect balance of consistency (righteousness) and completeness (perfection) between space and time. It is what qualifies Noah to come through the flood. *ch. 68*
- **Fathers** (The righteous one saved through the water): The one righteous man (with his household) kept alive through judgment to be the seed of a new world. *Ambrose, *De Sacr.* 2.1.1 — “What is the flood, but that in which the righteous is saved to be a seed-plot of righteousness, while sin dies?”; Ambrose, *De Sacr.* 2.1.1 — “he saved the righteous one alone with his offspring, but he bade the water rise even above the mountains.”; Ambrose, *De Myst.* 3.10 — “God, willing to restore what was lacking, sent the flood and bade just Noah go up into the ark.”*

</details>

### Guardian of the holy place  `guardian`

The one charged to keep the sanctuary and the bride from intruders: Adam first, then cherubim, priests, the Church.

**Key texts:** Gen 2:15; Gen 3:24; Num 3:7-8; Ezek 28:14
**Christ:** The good shepherd who guards the sheep against the wolf (John 10:11-12).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Guardian of the holy place): The office of guarding God's throne: Adam's job, passed to the cherubim after his failure, then to Levites, then to the Church. *ch. 9, p. 113*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Husband who must guard the bride): The covenant head charged with guarding the bride and the sanctuary against an invader. *RTD p.42*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Cherubim (throne-bearers and guardians)): Four-faced angels who bear God's throne-chariot, sing His holiness and guard His sanctuary, killing those who defile it. *ch. 9, p. 113*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Cherubim guardians): Angelic guards who replaced fallen man at the Garden gate and around God's throne. Their flaming sword marks exclusion. *ch. 11, p. 137*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Angelic guards who replace Adam): Guardians set at the garden after man fails to guard it, until human guardians are restored. *RTD p.46*
- **Pageau**, LoC (cherub / living creature): God's mount and support. As a hybrid sphinx it guards an unanswered enigma; as a square tetramorph it hosts the answered one. *ch. 40*
- **Pageau**, talks (Hybrid guardian / confusion of kinds): A creature that mixes several kinds. It stands for the loss of clear naming after the fall and guards the boundary of paradise. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/the-language-of-creation-matthieu-pageau-ep-292*

</details>

### The bride  `bride`

The woman placed in the garden, whom the serpent or tyrant tries to take, and whom the bridegroom washes and claims.

**Key texts:** Gen 2:22; Gen 12:14-20; Gen 24; Song 4:12; Eph 5:25-27; Rev 21:2
**Christ:** The Church is the bride, washed with water by the word (Eph 5:26).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Bride / Eve): The woman placed in the Garden to be guarded. The serpent assaults her in the exodus, and she is found at wells. *ch. 11, p. 136*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Bride under attack): The woman, or God's people, whom the serpent tries to take or defile in order to raise up his own seed. *RTD p.53*
- **Pageau**, LoC (the familiar woman / wife): A woman of the same law and people who reproduces the male's identity faithfully, giving consistent offspring. She is space in sexuality (Zillah). *ch. 72*
- **Fathers** (The bride (Church / soul)): The Church, or the baptized soul, prepared, washed and adorned for Christ the Bridegroom. *Cyril, *Cat.* 3.1 — “let the souls that are to be united to the spiritual Bridegroom make themselves ready.”; Cyril, *Cat.* 3.2 — “Begin at once to wash your robes in repentance, that when called to the bride-chamber ye may be found clean.”; Gregory, *Bapt. Chr.* (closing) — “the Adorner of the bride is Christ”*

</details>

### The bridegroom  `bridegroom`

The one who comes for the bride, washes and adorns her, and brings her into his house.

**Key texts:** Gen 24:63-67; Isa 62:5; Matt 25:1-13; John 3:29
**Christ:** John the Baptist: 'The one who has the bride is the bridegroom' (John 3:29).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Husband who must guard the bride): The covenant head charged with guarding the bride and the sanctuary against an invader. *RTD p.42*
- **Fathers** (The Bridegroom): Christ as the one who invites, washes and adorns the bride, and who also separates the unworthy guests. *Cyril, *Cat.* 3.2 — “For the Bridegroom invites all without distinction, because His grace is bounteous”; Cyril, *Cat.* 3.2 — “but the same Bridegroom afterwards separates those who have come in to the figurative marriage.”; Gregory, *Bapt. Chr.* (closing) — “the Adorner of the bride is Christ”*

</details>

### The tyrant  `tyrant`

The foreign lord who enslaves God's people, seizes the bride, is struck by God and drowned or overthrown.

**Key texts:** Gen 12:15; Exod 1:8-22; Exod 14:28; Dan 3; Matt 2:16
**Christ:** Herod plays Pharaoh at Christ's birth (Matt 2:16).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Tyrant / captor): The foreign lord of an exodus story. He enslaves God's people, seizes the bride, is plagued, blames the righteous, and loses gods and spoils. *ch. 14, p. 185*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Tyrant ruler who thinks himself a god): A foreign ruler who seizes the bride, breaks the rules of hospitality and oppresses God's people. *RTD p.54*
- **Jordan**, TNE (World emperor): The imperial ruler of the exile era who gathers the nations under one throne and protects or punishes God's people. *ch. 17, p. 250*
- **Fathers** (Pharaoh / the pursuing tyrant): An oppressor who pursues God's freed people to the water and is drowned there; read as the devil. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 9 — “the devil, their old tyrant, they leave quite behind, overwhelmed in the water.”; Cyril, *Myst.* 1.3 — “the tyrant was pursuing that ancient people even to the sea; and here the daring and shameless spirit, the author of evil”; Cyril, *Myst.* 1.3 — “The tyrant of old was drowned in the sea; and this present one disappears in the water of salvation.”*

</details>

### The deliverer sent  `deliverer`

A leader sent by God into the land of bondage to bring the people out through water.

**Key texts:** Exod 3:10; Judg 6:14; Acts 7:35
**Christ:** Cyril (Mystagogical Lecture 1): Moses sent to Egypt, Christ sent into the world.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Exodus (stage 2)): God moves His people from an old world to a new one through captivity, deliverance and spoils. It is the world-scale "restructuring" step. *ch. 13, p. 168*
- **Fathers** (Moses as the one sent): A leader sent by God into the land of bondage to bring an oppressed people out through water. *Cyril, *Myst.* 1.3 — “There we have Moses sent from God to Egypt; here, Christ, sent forth from His Father into the world”; Cyril, *Myst.* 1.3 — “here, that Christ might rescue those who are oppressed in the world under sin”; Ambrose, *De Sacr.* 5.1.3 — “Moses, that is to say, a prophet; the rod, that is the word of God.”*

</details>

### The forerunner  `forerunner`

The servant who goes ahead to prepare the way with repentance and water, and points to one greater.

**Key texts:** Isa 40:3; Mal 3:1; Mal 4:5; John 1:23; John 3:30
**Christ:** John the Baptist: 'He must increase, but I must decrease' (John 3:30).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Fathers** (The one who prepares the way): The servant who goes ahead, prepares by repentance and water, and points to the one greater who perfects. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 6 — “In this case also a type has preceded; for thus was John beforehand the Lord's forerunner”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 10 — “it conveyed nothing celestial, but it fore-ministered to things celestial”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 10 — “he who "prepares" does not himself "perfect," but procures for another to perfect.”*

</details>

### The younger son chosen  `younger-chosen`

The younger brother who receives the blessing in place of the firstborn.

**Key texts:** Gen 4:4-5; Gen 25:23; Gen 27; Gen 48:14; 1 Sam 16:11-13
**Part of:** `older-younger`

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Younger son who supplants): The younger brother who replaces the older heir. The older is a type of Adam, the younger a type of the Second Adam. *ch. 14, p. 188*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Younger over older): The firstborn forfeits by contempt or rebellion, and the blessing or rule passes to a younger son. *RTD p.57*
- **Pageau**, LoC (shepherd, priest (Abel, Jacob, Shem)): Adam specialized toward heaven: contemplative authority that offers refined flesh. Without the earthly twin he is "just breath" (Abel = vanity). *ch. 25*

</details>

### The firstborn passed over  `elder-passed-over`

The elder, often strong and earthly, who loses the blessing by contempt, violence or failure.

**Key texts:** Gen 4:5-8; Gen 25:29-34; Gen 49:3-4; Luke 15:25-32
**Part of:** `older-younger`

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Firstborn against younger): Which son inherits; in Genesis the failing firstborn is often passed over for a younger son. *RTD p.65*
- **Pageau**, LoC (farmer, king, builder (Cain, Esau, Ham)): Adam specialized toward earth: the power that holds land, builds cities and forges tools. He should host the heavenly twin, but often kills or exposes him. *ch. 25*

</details>

### The barren mother  `barren-mother`

The woman who cannot bear until God opens her womb, at a turning point of the story.

**Key texts:** Gen 18:10-14; Gen 25:21; Gen 30:22; 1 Sam 1; Luke 1:7
**Christ:** The line runs to Elizabeth and then to the virgin who conceives (Luke 1:36-37).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Son from a barren womb): A son given miraculously at a turning point. He restores the nation, and his birth is equivalent to building God's house. *ch. 14, p. 187*

</details>

### The stranger  `stranger`

The outsider who carries change into the story: the foreign woman, the spy, the captive maid, the Gentile convert.

**Key texts:** Josh 2; Ruth 1:16; 2 Kgs 5:2-3; Matt 1:3-6; Matt 15:21-28
**Christ:** Tamar, Rahab and Ruth stand in Christ's genealogy (Matt 1:3-5).
**Where the readings differ:** Pageau: the strange woman brings ruin and renewal. Ambrose: the captive maid is the Church of the Gentiles.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Noahic Gentile / God-fearer): A converted Gentile who stays under the Noahic covenant, worships the Most High, blesses Israel, and lives outside the holy land. *ch. 15, p. 211*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Converted Gentile ruler): A pagan lord who is warned by a vision or converted, then favours God's people and sets them over his realm. *ch. 14, p. 192*
- **Pageau**, LoC (stranger / other / foreigner): The flip side of a revealed identity: whatever does not express the Name. It is a carrier of transformation (spies, foreign nations, strange gods). *ch. 52*
- **Pageau**, LoC (the strange woman / harlot): A foreign, unbound woman who does not reproduce the seed faithfully. She means ruin and also renewal (Adah, Rahab, Tamar, Sheba). *ch. 75*
- **Fathers** (The captive girl (the Gentile Church)): A captive foreign girl who points her master to the prophet and the healing water; read as the Gentile Church. *Ambrose, *De Myst.* 3.18 — “She is the congregation gathered out of the Gentiles, that is, the Church of God held down of old by the captivity of sin”; Ambrose, *De Sacr.* 2.3.8 — “one who had the likeness of the Church, and exhibited a figure of it”; Ambrose, *De Sacr.* 2.3.8 — “For the people of the nations was captive.”*

</details>

### Man of the margin  `man-of-the-margin`

A strong figure of the edge, marked by hair, skins or exile: hunter, wanderer, prophet. Power or holiness, depending on use.

**Key texts:** Gen 4:15-16; Gen 25:25-27; 2 Kgs 1:8; Matt 3:4
**Christ:** John the Baptist in camel's hair at the Jordan (Matt 3:4).
**Where the readings differ:** This is Pageau's category; Jordan does not name it.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (Covered, wild figure on the edge (Cain, Esau, Nimrod, Elijah)): A strong figure marked by hair or skins, living at the edge (hunter, wanderer, prophet), who carries the garments of skin. Ambivalent: power or witness. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yo8TrzRm6J4*
- **Pageau**, talks (The scapegoat cast to the edge): A figure expelled and marked as outside, who wanders in hostile land. The exclusion also founds identity and builds a city. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gh9BC-wONBs*
- **Pageau**, talks (Smith/technician of the dark edge): The maker or smith tied to the underground and the marginal (Cain's line, Hephaestus, dwarves). He gathers dark potential into tools and weapons. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CS5Asc29oaY*

</details>

### The fool  `fool`

A low or upside-down figure who exposes the king's nakedness; in the good case the low one raised up.

**Key texts:** 1 Sam 21:13; 2 Sam 6:14-22; 1 Cor 1:27; 1 Cor 4:10
**Christ:** The mock king crowned with thorns (Matt 27:27-31).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (The inversion figure): A marginal, upside-down figure who exposes the king's nakedness. In the positive case (David), he is the low one raised up, foreshadowing Christ's reversal. *https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxltkn90Qag*
- **Pageau**, LoC (fool / jester / clown): Time as a human type: idle, playful, deceiving, inverting. He "eats his own flesh" and loosens what is bound, which brings both ruin and renewal. *ch. 46*
- **Pageau**, talks (Licensed upside-down time, then death and order): For a set time the margin takes the center (masks, noise, monsters, a mock king), ending in humiliation, death or fasting, then restored order. *https://orthodoxartsjournal.org/jordan-peterson-religious-symbolism-and-popular-culture/; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxltkn90Qag*

</details>

### The city-builder  `city-builder`

The rebel who takes creation without thanks and builds a city and a name for himself.

**Key texts:** Gen 4:17; Gen 10:8-12; Gen 11:4; Dan 4:30

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (City-builder (Cain / Nimrod)): The rebel who takes creation without thanks and builds a city of man as a counterfeit sanctuary. *ch. 13, p. 178*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Mighty men / Nephilim): Violent rulers who build a single world state. They were the "heavens" of the pre-Flood world, and Nimrod tried to bring them back at Babel. *ch. 13, p. 175*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Tyranny grows from youth to age): Abused office grows from murder to a city to boasted violence to mixing with the godly, until judgment. *RTD p.47*

</details>

### The servant raised up  `servant-raised`

The faithful servant who is stripped, enslaved or imprisoned, serves well from below, and is robed by the king.

**Key texts:** Gen 39-41; Dan 1-2; Dan 6; Esth 6:11; Phil 2:7-9
**Christ:** Taking the form of a servant, he humbled himself; therefore God highly exalted him (Phil 2:7-9).

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Faithful servant who gains mastery): A servant who, through humble and competent service under unjust masters, comes to rule in fact and later in name. *RTD p.67*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Faithful witness at court): A servant of the pagan empire who serves it well while openly bearing witness to God. He does not compromise and does not rebel. *ch. 17, p. 250*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (From pit to throne through service): Stripped, thrown down and enslaved, the faithful servant rules from below, refuses illicit power, interprets God's word and is robed by the king. *RTD p.67*

</details>

### Priest, king and prophet  `priest-king-prophet`

Three offices of the human mediator: the priest guards, the king rules and builds, the prophet stands in God's council.

**Key texts:** Gen 14:18; Deut 18:18; Ps 110:4; Heb 1:1-3
**Christ:** Christ holds all three offices; Tertullian: the name 'Christ' comes from the anointing of priests and kings.

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Priest (priestly or "hieratic" office)): The guardian who recognizes holy boundaries and keeps them, mainly by leading people in worship. *ch. 11, p. 136*
- **Jordan**, TNE (King (kingly or "basilic" office)): The ruler who first understands and names the world and then beautifies it. Kingship is exercised through service. *ch. 11, p. 135*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Prophet (divine council member)): A member of God's council whom God consults. The prophet argues before God and announces the council's decisions to the people. *ch. 11, p. 138*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Prophet as one who stands between): The one who speaks God's word into the world and speaks for two opposed sides, given to man after the Fall. *RTD p.40 fn2*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Priest-king (Noahic polity)): The ruler of a post-Flood city-state who holds sacred and judicial authority together over one of the separated nations. *ch. 13, p. 175*
- **Leithart**, talks (Stages of maturity): Humanity and Israel mature from priestly obedience to kingly rule to prophetic counsel, a pattern also found in single lives and crafts. *https://theopolisinstitute.com/conversations/models-mentors-and-rules-of-reading/*
- **Fathers** (The washed-then-anointed priest and king): One washed in water and then anointed with oil to be priest or king; the name "Christ" comes from this. *Tertullian, *Bapt.* 7 — “Whence Aaron is called "Christ," from the "chrism," which is "the unction;"”; Tertullian, *Bapt.* 7 — “the unction runs carnally, (i.e. on the body,) but profits spiritually”; Cyril, *Myst.* 3.6 — “Aaron was called Christ or Anointed, evidently from the typical Chrism.”*

</details>

## Acts

One step in a story.

### Separate  `separate`

Divide one thing from another to make room for order: light from dark, waters from waters, a people from the nations.

**Key texts:** Gen 1:4-7; Exod 14:21; Lev 20:24; Josh 3:16

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Word, act, naming, witness, judgment): God's five-step creating pattern: command, separate or fill, name, see, judge "good." *RTD p.39*
- **Jordan**, TNE (Covenant-cutting as dividing and restructuring): To make a covenant is to cut something apart and rejoin it in a new form, which then gets a new name. *ch. 11, p. 136*
- **Pageau**, LoC (separating / establishing space): Holding opposites apart by removing the intermediate states, so that certainty replaces confusion. Any separation (milk from meat, linen from wool) "establishes space". *ch. 37*

</details>

### Name  `name`

Give a creature or person its identity by speaking. Naming comes before rule.

**Key texts:** Gen 1:5; Gen 2:19-20; Gen 17:5; Gen 32:28; Matt 16:18

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Jordan**, TNE (Symbol first, then reality): God and man name or define first and then make; God declares us righteous, and then remakes us. *ch. 3, p. 33*
- **Jordan**, SM/RTD (Name first, then rule): Symbol comes before reality: God declares and it happens; man names his intention and then puts it into effect. *SM p.3*
- **Pageau**, LoC (naming): Lowering heaven into earth through breath: giving a creature its spiritual identity. It goes with eating and taming. *ch. 17*
- **Pageau**, talks (See, name, judge): Something appears as a puzzle, light identifies it, speech names it, and judgment declares it good, bringing it into ordered participation. *https://rosetta.to/u/jordanbpeterson/a-lecture-by-jonathan-pageau-the-symbolic-world-ep-206; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1-vG-bLDwE:*

</details>

### Go down  `descend`

Go down into water, pit, sleep, Egypt or death. Also used for heaven coming down.

**Key texts:** Gen 12:10; Gen 46:3-4; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 2:6; Matt 3:16
**Opposite:** `ascend`

<details><summary>Readings by tradition</summary>

- **Pageau**, talks (Pattern brought down from above): Meaning comes down from heaven and is impressed on matter: light on the waters, a name on an animal, a pattern on tabernacle materials. *https://adversariapodcast.com/2019/03/07/transcript-for-matthieu-pageau-the-language-of-creation/*
- **Pageau**, LoC (materialization): A heavenly seed comes down and is made tangible: breath into word, dew into frost, flour into bread, name into laws. *ch. 16*
