---

## Author profile

**Trigger.** On the reader, next to the author name (Augustine, etc.), a small info control. Tap it. Do not replace the author picker — sit beside it.

**What opens.** A profile for that Father, not a Wikipedia dump. Enough to know who you are reading.

### Globe (STL)

- Full globe, 3D, STL (or equivalent mesh) of the earth.
- Highlight their origin (Hippo, Antioch, …).
- If we have it: the **journey of their life** as a path on the globe — places they lived, preached, were exiled, died.

### Timeline (bottom row)

- A scrubbable timeline under the globe.
- Points on the map and ticks on the timeline are the same events.
- Tap a point: where they were, when, and **which works they wrote then**.
- Goal: more context on the person, not just the text.

### Key events / the world at the time

- Second tab (or pane) on the profile: **what else was happening** when this was written.
- Empires, councils, heresies, wars, translations — only what helps the reader.
- Tied to the same timeline as the globe, so a Liber and a world event can sit on one date.

**Data we do not have yet.** Birth/death, sees, exile routes, work dates, “world events.” This feature needs a small biography dataset per Father before any 3D work. Log the UI first; corpus second.
