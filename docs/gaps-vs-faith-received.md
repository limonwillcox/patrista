# Gap list: Patrista against The Faith Received

What Mere Orthodoxy's library, The Faith Received, holds from the patristic era that Patrista does not, Father by Father.

- Built 2026-11-14 from their public catalogue (Greek, Latin and Eastern shelves) and Patrista's built catalogue (`dist/api/catalog.json`).
- Era: Migne's Greek volumes 1–96 (to John of Damascus) and Latin volumes 1–96 (to the late 8th century). Their Eastern shelf is listed whole, since it carries no dates.
- Every piece, with its link, is in [gaps-vs-faith-received.csv](gaps-vs-faith-received.csv).

## Read this first

- **Their counts are pieces, not works.** A piece can be a whole commentary, one homily, or a fragment. Patrista's 355 works are mostly whole books and whole letter collections. Use the counts to rank, not to measure.
- **Their English is machine translation**, and for the Greek Fathers it is made from Migne's Latin column. A gap here means "they show a text Patrista does not", not "a translation exists to copy".
- **Title matching in section 2 is approximate.** It compares title words. A piece marked as having no match may still sit inside a Patrista work under another name. Check before sourcing.
- Editors' prefaces, notices, indexes and title pages are left out of the counts. Pieces of doubtful authorship are counted separately.
- **Some Greek pieces are titled only with the author's name.** Some of these hold that author's works in one file (Justin Martyr is one piece), others are a short notice. They are counted, but left out of the examples and the no-match lists.

## How this was built

1. Their three catalogue files were read as published: one row per piece with author heading, Migne volume and title.
2. Author headings were folded onto one name per Father by a hand-written table (for example "Cyril", "Cyrillus Alexandrinus" and "Cyril of Alexandria"). Headings that name a modern editor or a whole anthology volume were set aside.
3. Latin pieces their catalogue marks as editorial were dropped. Greek pieces were dropped when the title reads as a title page, index, preface or notice. That Greek filter is a word list, so a few editorial pieces remain and a few real ones may be lost.
4. Fathers were matched to Patrista's by name. Titles were compared by shared words after removing filler and folding common synonyms.
5. Clavis figures come from `data/clavis/works.jsonl`. Authors with a single row there are stubs and are left blank.
