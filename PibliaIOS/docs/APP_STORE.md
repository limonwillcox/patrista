# App Store notes

Do this on a Mac with Xcode and an Apple Developer account. Not from this sandbox.

1. Enroll at developer.apple.com — $99 / year
2. Bundle ID: `com.piblia.app` (change if taken)
3. App Store Connect → new app → iOS
4. Privacy: v1 stores bookmarks and Latin/focus on device only. No account. No tracking.
5. Icon 1024×1024 PNG, no transparency
6. Screenshots: 6.7-inch and 6.1-inch (reader, Liber sheet, focus)
7. Support URL: https://patrista.com
8. Marketing URL: https://patrista.com/get-app.html
9. Then paste the App Store URL into `get-app.html` (`#appStoreBtn`) and remove `aria-disabled`

Review copy, short: “Read the Church Fathers on your iPhone. Confessions of Saint Augustine, English and Latin.”
