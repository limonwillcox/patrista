   of the Synod of CÃ¦sarea. [3785]

   Endeavour also to send abroad copies of our epistle among all the
   churches, so that those who easily deceive their own souls may not be
   able to lay the blame on us.  We would have you know, too, that in
   Alexandria [3786] also they observe the festival on the same day as
   ourselves.  For the Paschal letters are sent from us to them, and from
   them to us:  so that we observe the holy day in unison and together.
